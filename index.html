Protocols, not policy.

Nunki handles transport and protocol behavior. Your host still chooses prompts, context, tools, edits, and where credentials belong.

Provider-neutral

Stream OpenAI-compatible and Anthropic APIs through common messages and structured parts.

MCP complete

Use tools, resources, prompts, and pagination over stdio or Streamable HTTP.

Explicit limits

Bound requests, responses, retries, timeouts, and context before crossing a network boundary.

Open the stream.

Choose a provider and supply the full endpoint from your own configuration.

Ruby
$ bundle add nunki

provider = Nunki::Provider.build(
  :openai,
  endpoint: ENV.fetch("LLM_ENDPOINT"),
  api_key: ENV.fetch("LLM_API_KEY"),
  model: "configured-model"
